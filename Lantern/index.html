"In brightest day, in blackest night, no evil shall escape my sight. Let those who worship evil's might, beware my power, Green Lantern's light!"

~Hal Jordan

I'm Hal Jordan, a test pilot and member of the Green Lantern Corps, an intergalactic peacekeeping force that protects the universe. As a Green Lantern, I help defend Earth and Sector 2814 from threats across the galaxy, fighting alongside thousands of Lanterns to maintain justice and order.
The Green Lantern Ring is one of the most powerful weapons in the DC Universe. Powered by willpower, it allows its wielder to create almost anything they can imagine. Each ring chooses a worthy bearer and helps the Green Lantern Corps protect the universe.
